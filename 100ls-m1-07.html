📜 Dialog Script
EmmaHi, can I get a medium latte, please?
BaristaSure! Do you want that hot or iced?
EmmaIced, please. Can you add an extra shot?
BaristaOf course. Any milk preference?
EmmaOat milk, if you have it.
BaristaWe do! That'll be five fifty. Name for the order?
EmmaEmma. Oh, and can I get a sleeve on that?
BaristaAbsolutely. It'll be ready in just a minute.
EmmaThanks so much.
BaristaYou're welcome! Have a great day!
Emma: 你好，我要一杯中杯拿铁，谢谢。
Barista: 好的！您想要热的还是冰的？
Emma: 冰的，谢谢。能加一份浓缩吗？
Barista: 当然可以。有什么牛奶偏好吗？
Emma: 燕麦奶，如果有的话。
Barista: 有的！一共五块五。请问您叫什么名字？
Emma: Emma。哦，能给我套个杯套吗？
Barista: 没问题，马上就好，大概一分钟。
Emma: 非常感谢。
Barista: 不客气！祝您愉快！
